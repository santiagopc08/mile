# RA-002 — Gameplay Design

Version: 1.0 (Draft)  
Status: Draft  
Category: Reference Application

---

## Core Gameplay Loop

```text
Start
  ↓
Accelerate
  ↓
Move
  ↓
Collect Coins
  ↓
Consume Fuel
  ↓
Travel Distance
  ↓
Refuel
  ↓
Continue
  ↓
Crash
  ↓
Restart
```

---

## Player Goals

**Primary:**

Travel as far as possible.

**Secondary:**

- Collect coins.
- Manage fuel.
- Avoid flipping.

---

## Failure Conditions

- Vehicle upside down.
- Fuel reaches zero.
- Vehicle destroyed.

---

## Victory

There is no ending.

Distance is the primary score.

---

## Controls

- Accelerate
- Brake
- Pause
- Restart
- Camera Reset

---

## Gameplay Metrics

- Distance
- Coins
- Fuel
- Speed
- Air Time
- Rotation
- Maximum Speed
- Average Speed
